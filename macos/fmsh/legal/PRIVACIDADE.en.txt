FM SEASON HUB — PRIVACY POLICY
Version 1.3 — in force from the release of version 0.7.2 of the Program

This Policy sets out the personal data processing operations carried out by FM
Season Hub, the corresponding purposes and legal bases, the parties involved and
the rights afforded to the data subject. It applies to the program installed on
the user's computer, to its installer and to the remote service they consult.

Controller: FM Season Hub, an independent project established in Brazil.
Contact channel for privacy matters: fmseasonhub@gmail.com

## 1. Definitions

- **Data subject** — the natural person to whom the personal data processed
  relate.
- **Processing** — any operation carried out with personal data, as defined in
  art. 5, X of the LGPD and art. 4(2) of the GDPR.
- **Controller** — FM Season Hub, which determines the purposes and means of
  processing.
- **Processor** — a party carrying out processing on the Controller's behalf.
- **Program** — the FM Season Hub application installed on the data subject's
  device.
- **Installer** — the wizard that installs, updates and removes the Program and,
  where chosen by the data subject, the plugins for the game.
- **FM Season Hub Service** — the remote service operated by the Controller that
  confirms the access level, publishes notices and distributes the Program's
  updates.
- **Device identifier** — the cryptographic digest defined in clause 5.1.

## 2. Data not processed by the Controller

Data extracted from the data subject's game file — players, clubs, competitions,
statistics, attributes and financial information — **is not transmitted to the
Controller or to any third party**, and remains exclusively on the data subject's
device.

The Program performs no collection of browsing data, telemetry, behavioural
profiling or advertising. The data subject's operations within the Program are
not recorded outside the device.

The Program and the Installer establish eight types of external connection, set
out in clause 5: seven with the FM Season Hub Service and one with the official
website of the BepInEx project. No other communication with the network takes
place.

## 3. Data stored on the data subject's device

The data subject's data is held in two directories on the device itself, under
the data subject's exclusive control.

In the Program's data directory, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\FM Season Hub\dados:

- the database of captured seasons, and working copies of it that are rebuilt
  automatically when deleted;
- the internal profile of the display component;
- copies of the latest responses from the FM Season Hub Service (notices,
  available version and names in the credits).

In the Documents\FM Season Hub directory:

- the preferences file, containing language, currency, list of favourites, access
  key where one exists, the device identifier defined in clause 5.1 and the
  record of acceptance of these documents (version accepted, date and language),
  given in the Program or in the Installer;
- the event log (fmsh.log), for diagnostic purposes;
- the filters, views and translations created by the data subject, and the
  reports of the diagnostic program;
- the update packages obtained as set out in clauses 5.6 and 5.8, deleted after
  installation.

The Program's own files are installed in the
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\FM Season Hub directory and contain no personal data.

Deleting both data directories results in the complete erasure of the data held
on the device.

## 4. Cookies and local storage

The Program **uses no tracking, advertising or behavioural analytics cookies**,
and no third-party cookies.

The display component retains on the data subject's device only the data required
to persist interface preferences:

- **local storage** — language, currency, selected columns, favourites and
  display preferences, retained until erased by the data subject;
- **display component profile** — window state, retained until erased by the data
  subject.

That data is neither transmitted to nor accessed by the Controller.

## 5. Data transmitted to the FM Season Hub Service

### 5.1 Device identifier

In order to prevent the unrestricted circulation of access keys, the Program
computes a device identifier and transmits it to the FM Season Hub Service
together with the key.

The identifier consists of a cryptographic digest (SHA-256) derived from the
operating system installation identifier combined with the local user name. Only
the digest is transmitted — a 32-character string from which neither the user
name nor any other device data can be reconstructed.

Purpose: to allow the same access to be used on up to two devices and to prevent
improper sharing.

### 5.2 Access key

The key entered by the data subject is transmitted for verification. The Program
performs periodic automatic re-verification in order to establish whether the
access remains valid. Where no connection is available, the last verified level
prevails; access is not withdrawn by reason of network unavailability.

### 5.3 Authentication with Patreon

Where the data subject elects to authenticate with Patreon, that platform
communicates to the Controller, upon express authorisation given in Patreon's own
environment, the data subject's name and the active support levels.

The following are processed by the Controller: the data subject's name, the name
of the support level, the corresponding level within the Program, and the refresh
token enabling re-verification of support without further authentication.

The Controller has no access to payment, card or address data, which remains the
responsibility of Patreon.

### 5.4 Notice board

The Program periodically retrieves the list of notices published by the
Controller. That request carries no personal data of the data subject, and is
limited to the Program version.

### 5.5 Supporter list shown in the credits

The Program periodically retrieves the list of names published in the credits,
which is the subject of clause 8. That request carries no personal data of the
data subject, and is limited to the Program version.

### 5.6 Checking for and obtaining updates

The Program asks the FM Season Hub Service whether a new version exists: upon
opening and, during use, every 12 hours. Where a new version exists and the
Program was installed by the Installer, the installation package is obtained from
the same service and installed automatically, after the authenticity check
described in clause 13.

Those requests carry no personal data of the data subject, and are limited to the
Program version. The Controller keeps only counts added up by version and by day
(checks received and packages obtained), with no link to the IP address, device
identifier or any other data of the data subject, as set out in clause 5.9.

### 5.7 Obtaining BepInEx through the Installer

Where the data subject chooses to install plugins into a game installation that
lacks the BepInEx component, the Installer obtains it directly from the official
website of the BepInEx project (builds.bepinex.dev) and verifies the integrity of
the file before using it.

That request carries no personal data of the data subject and does not pass
through the Controller, which neither receives nor records any information about
it. As with any network communication, the originating IP address is visible to
the party responsible for that website, under its own policy.

### 5.8 Checking for and obtaining plugin updates

For the plugins installed by the Installer, the Program asks the FM Season Hub
Service for the list of available versions (the catalogue): upon opening and,
during use, every 12 hours. In that request the Program reports the name and
version of each installed plugin. Where a newer version exists that is
compatible with the Game, the package is obtained from the same service and
installed only while the Game is closed, after the authenticity check described
in clause 13. The previous version is kept on the device for restoration, which
happens automatically if the Game does not load the new plugin, or at the data
subject's request.

The Program never installs a plugin that the data subject has not chosen. The
data subject may switch off automatic updating for each plugin on the "Game
plugins" screen. Plugins are updated only after this version of the Policy has
been accepted.

Those requests carry no personal data of the data subject and no device
identifier.

### 5.9 Anonymous usage counts

To know how many installations receive each version of the Program and of each
plugin, the FM Season Hub Service adds up, each day, the requests received per
version (for example, "version 0.7.2: 120 checks"). Who made the request is not
recorded: no IP address, device identifier, name or per-device list of plugins is
stored, and the count cannot show how many distinct people exist. The counts
serve only to follow the adoption of versions and to plan gradual releases.

## 6. Data collected automatically by the FM Season Hub Service

The FM Season Hub Service is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. As a technical
consequence of any network communication, the originating IP address is visible
to that company and appears in its access logs.

The Controller uses the IP address for a single, determined purpose: to count
invalid key attempts and to prevent bulk guessing. That record is erased
automatically after 15 minutes. The IP address is not used in the requests
covered by clauses 5.4 to 5.6, 5.8 and 5.9.

## 7. Sharing with third parties

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** — hosts the FM Season Hub Service and the update files,
  and stores the records described in this Policy, acting as a **Processor** on
  the Controller's behalf.
- **BepInEx project** — maintains the website from which the Installer obtains
  BepInEx (clause 5.7). It receives no data of the data subject from the
  Controller.
- **Patreon, Inc.** — source of support data where the data subject
  authenticates. The scope of sharing is defined in the authorisation presented
  by that platform.
- **Competent authorities** — where a legal or regulatory obligation applies.

The Controller **does not sell, assign or otherwise make available** personal data
to third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

## 8. Publication of supporter names

The Program displays, on its About screen, the names of supporters on the tier
whose description expressly provides for a mention in the credits.

- **Data published** — solely the nickname chosen by the data subject on Patreon
  and, where none has been set, the public name given there. No email address,
  pledge amount, join date or any other datum is published.
- **Legal basis** — performance of a contract at the data subject's request
  (LGPD art. 7, V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)): the mention in the credits forms part of
  the consideration for the contracted tier, and is not presumed consent.
- **Removal** — a data subject who has ticked, on Patreon, the option to hide
  their own pledge is automatically excluded from the list. Removal may also be
  requested at any time through the channel in clause 16, taking effect within
  6 (six) hours, the temporary storage period dealt with in clause 11.
- **Scope** — the list is public and accessible to any user of the Program.

## 9. International transfers

Cloudflare and Patreon are legal entities established outside Brazil, and the
data described in this Policy is processed on servers abroad.

The transfer relies on the grounds set out in art. 33 of the LGPD and, where
applicable, on the corresponding mechanisms in Chapter V of the GDPR.

## 10. Legal bases for processing

- **Performance of a contract** (LGPD art. 7, V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) —
  verification of the contracted access level, distribution of the Program's
  updates and publication of the name in the credits, where the contracted tier
  provides for it.
- **Legitimate interests** (LGPD art. 7, IX; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) — prevention of
  improper key sharing and of bulk guessing.
- **Consent** (LGPD art. 7, I; GDPR art. 6(1)(a)) — authentication with Patreon.

The consent referred to in the last of these is optional and specific, given by
the data subject in Patreon's environment and revocable at any time on that
platform.

## 11. Retention periods

- Access record — level, name, refresh token and device identifiers: for as long
  as the access subsists. There is no automatic erasure deadline; records are
  erased upon the data subject's request or upon termination of the access.
- Name published in the credits: for as long as the support subsists on the
  corresponding tier. The temporary storage of the list is refreshed every
  6 hours.
- Attempt counter per IP address: 15 minutes.
- Temporary Patreon authentication authorisation: 5 minutes.
- Counts added up by version and by day (checks and packages obtained, for the
  Program and the plugins): indefinitely, as they contain no personal data.
- Data held on the device: until erased by the data subject; update packages,
  until the Program is next opened after their installation.

## 12. Rights of the data subject

Under art. 18 of the LGPD, the data subject is entitled to request at any time:
confirmation that processing takes place; access to the data; correction of
incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data; anonymisation, blocking or erasure of
data that is unnecessary or processed unlawfully; portability; information as to
sharing; and withdrawal of consent.

Data subjects established in the European Union are further entitled to object to
and to restrict processing (GDPR arts. 18 and 21), and to lodge a complaint with
the competent supervisory authority.

Requests are to be addressed to fmseasonhub@gmail.com. The response period is up
to 15 (fifteen) days.

Independently of any request, the data subject has the following direct means
available:

- signing out of the account within the Program, which removes the local link;
- deleting the Documents\FM Season Hub and
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\FM Season Hub\dados directories, which completely
  erases the data held on the device;
- uninstalling the Program, which removes its files and, where the data subject
  so chooses in the uninstaller itself, the plugins installed by the Installer,
  while keeping both data directories;
- revoking the authorisation granted to FM Season Hub in the Patreon account
  settings, which stops the sharing at source.

## 13. Security

Communications with the FM Season Hub Service take place over an encrypted
channel (HTTPS). The access key and the device identifiers are held in storage
restricted to the service, and the device identifier subsists solely in the form
of a cryptographic digest.

Update packages are accompanied by the Controller's digital signature. The
Program verifies the signature and the integrity of each package before
installing it, and rejects anything that fails verification.

No system is entirely secure. Should a security incident occur that may give rise
to relevant risk or harm to the data subject, the Controller will notify the data
subject and the competent authority as required by art. 48 of the LGPD and
arts. 33 and 34 of the GDPR.

## 14. Children

The Program is not intended for children under 13, and no data of that age group
is knowingly collected. Paid features require a Patreon account, a platform which
sets its own minimum age rules.

## 15. Amendments to this Policy

Where this Policy is amended, the version stated in the preamble will change and
the Program will request acceptance again on first execution following the
update.

The documents form part of the installation package; a new version is received by
installing a new version of the Program, including through automatic updates.

## 16. Data protection officer and contact

No data protection officer has been formally appointed. The functions set out in
art. 41, § 2 of the LGPD are performed directly by the Controller, through
fmseasonhub@gmail.com.

## 17. Entry into force

This Policy takes effect on the date stated in the preamble and remains in force
until replaced by a subsequent version.

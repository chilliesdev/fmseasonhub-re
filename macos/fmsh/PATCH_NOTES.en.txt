 v0.7.5 alpha — 13 Oct 2026
====================================================

FIND PLAYERS LIKE
---------------------------------------------------
[NOVO] Find players like: right-click a player (or use
   the "Find players like…" button on the profile) and
   FMSH finds players similar to him. There are two
   kinds of search, and each measures something
   different.
[NOVO] Replacement: what the player delivers
   (goals, assists, chances created and similar),
   at a similar level and much cheaper. It accepts
   players who deliver the same or more, with CA
   from 85% to 115% of his, and value and wage up
   to 70% and up to his. The table shows a Saving
   column.
[NOVO] Playing style: how the player plays (passes,
   tackles, fouls, runs), up and down, in any
   league, level or price.
[NOVO] Each search panel lets you adjust the
   tolerance, how many metrics may fall outside
   the range, the minutes, the quality floor and
   ceiling, value, age, contract end, the top 20
   leagues and the preferred foot (by footprints).
[NOVO] The result shows on the Seasons page, with
   the similarity, each metric's difference from
   the player's, and his row pinned at the top.
   The Columns… button lets you add any statistic.
[NOVO] Average profile: with 2 or 3 players
   selected, the search uses their average.
[NOVO] Recipes: a search can be saved to the
   Library (.rsh file) and applied to another
   season or shared.
[NOVO] In Settings, the Find players like section sets
   which restrictions the panel opens with.

PLAYER PROFILE
---------------------------------------------------
[NOVO] The Player Data tab gained the Goalkeeper
   tab and the goalkeeper Overview: saves, goals
   prevented, footwork and what matters most to
   someone in goal.
[CORRIGIDO] Players generated by the game during a
   save (newgens) show their photo again and open
   correctly in the profile from the game.
[NOVO] Open in FM: on the profile, in the
   comparison (one button per player) and on the
   tables' right-click menu, it opens the player
   in the running FM. Needs the updated FMSH
   in-game plugin.
[NOVO] The Player Data tab shows inside the player
   profile in FM itself, in any skin, with the
   season picker (FMSH in-game plugin).
[NOVO] In the Player Data inside FM you can mark the
   player as a favourite, and the mark shows right
   away in the FMSH window.
[CORRIGIDO] FM24: the Player Data tab now shows the
   data from each match (match by match, role in
   the team, performance by context and splits),
   also in the live reading.
[MUDOU] When a player's per-match data is missing,
   the notice says why: game version, matches the
   game already deleted or a season without games.
[CORRIGIDO] After the Euros, Copa América or AFCON,
   the player's season counts his club games
   again, not the national team ones.
[MUDOU] In FM24, which does not accept the FMSH
   plugins, the Open in FM button does not show.
[NOVO] Player Data inside FM follows the skin:
   graphite background and cards in MrTini; its
   gradient and dark cards in CTRL 26; the original

[MUDOU] Player Data inside FM uses the language
   chosen in FMSH and switches without restarting.
[MUDOU] A player from a league the game does not

SEASONS
---------------------------------------------------
[NOVO] Auto snapshot: when your club's league and
   cups end, FMSH saves the season by itself. Turn
   it on or off in Settings, with a name template
   and the choice between warning or replacing the
   oldest one when the slots are full.
[NOVO] Season Tracker: leagues on another calendar
   are read right after they end, and the Database
   shows, for each player, his latest complete
   season. When the number is not from the saved
   season, a tag next to the name says so (for

[NOVO] In Archived seasons, the two blocks (Auto
   snapshot and Season Tracker) show the dates and
   have a Configure shortcut.
[MUDOU] A notice over the game announces the Auto
   snapshot, with sound; the Season Tracker shows
   only a small pill, without sound.

DATABASE
---------------------------------------------------
[CORRIGIDO] A filter you removed from the Database

[CORRIGIDO] FM24: the youth teams filter now

WINDOWS TRAY
---------------------------------------------------
[NOVO] FMSH can stay in the Windows tray, next to

[NOVO] The icon shows the connection: green dot,

   1.2. They are accepted in the installer, and the

   remove the plugins and BepInEx it installed. Your
   seasons are kept.

PERFORMANCE
---------------------------------------------------
[MUDOU] Opening a saved season went from many
   seconds (minutes, on a hard drive) to under 1
   second.
[MUDOU] The player profile opens in about 2 seconds
   the first time, and instantly after that.
[MUDOU] Connecting to the game is about twice as
   fast, and the Database is ready as soon as the
   read ends.
[MUDOU] Going back to a screen or a season you
   already opened is instant, with no loading window

   between seasons instant.
[INTERNO] Each saved season gets a ready-to-open

   folder. Deleting the season deletes the copy too.
   While the app is idle, FMSH prepares these copies

[INTERNO] When connecting, the player read uses
   several processor cores at once — CPU usage going
   up for a few seconds is expected.

THE LANGUAGE BECAME A FILE
---------------------------------------------------

   the result — no need to wait for a new version.
[NOVO] Settings > Preferences now lists the languages,
   how much of each is translated, a button that
   generates the file to translate and another that
   opens the folder. The file comes with the English
   next to every phrase, so the translator knows what
   they are translating.
[NOVO] To install a language somebody sent you, copy
   their folder into the Idiomas folder. It shows up in
   the list on its own.
[MUDOU] A phrase that has not been translated yet shows
   in ENGLISH, not Portuguese. English is now the
   reference translators work from.

ADVANCED FILTER
---------------------------------------------------
[CORRIGIDO] Several advanced-filter fields stayed in
   Portuguese in the other languages — preferred foot,
   contract type and over 300 column names. They all
   follow the chosen language now, and they all go into
   the files you can correct.

CURRENCY
---------------------------------------------------
[CORRIGIDO] On a fresh install the currency picker kept
   saying "connect to FM" even after connecting and

SAVE SEASON
---------------------------------------------------

PLAYER PROFILE
---------------------------------------------------
[CORRIGIDO] The "Career" option, which adds up every
   season, did not show up even with two seasons saved.
   The app decided whether the season had rolled over by

   dropped and the two counted as one. It now compares

[NOVO] A POSITION PICKER drawn as a football pitch — ST at
   the top, GK at the bottom, 14 positions. Two things
   FMST doesn't do:
     · the cell has THREE states, not two, because the
       game stores familiarity from 1 to 20 and not a
       "plays / doesn't play";
     · the threshold is adjustable (default 15,
       "Competent"). Without it, "plays at ST" would bring
       in anyone with 3 out of 20 there.
   Sweeper was left out: ZERO of the save's 376 thousand
   players has that familiarity — the cell would exist
   only to never return anyone.

[NOVO] AND / OR between conditions, with ONE level of
   brackets. You can ask for things like
   "value ≥ 1m AND (age ≤ 21 OR age ≥ 34)".

[NOVO] A plain-English summary above the list, with the
   brackets written out. It isn't decoration: there is no
   OR precedence, so "A AND B OR C" and "A AND (B OR C)"
   are different things, and looking only at the rows that
   is invisible.

[NOVO] A condition can be TURNED OFF without being deleted
   — it drops out of the filter and comes back whenever
   you want.

[NOVO] New operators: not equal to, not known, contains /
   starts with / ends with (text), and comparing one field
   against ANOTHER field (e.g. PA greater than CA).

[NOVO] Field search: there are ~680 per tab, so the picker
   has a search box and sections.

[MUDOU] The old filter still works. It is read once to
   bring across what you already had, and it is NOT
   deleted.

[CORRIGIDO] A condition on a column that doesn't apply to
   that position was applied anyway. The game stores
   "tackles attempted" and "penalties faced" in the SAME
   place in memory, so in a keeper the sum came out under
   the wrong name. Now the column isn't even offered, and
